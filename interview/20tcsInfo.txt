### About Tata Consultancy Services

**Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)** is one of the world’s leading IT services, consulting, and business solutions companies. It is part of the well-known **Tata Group**, which is one of India’s largest and most trusted business groups.

---

### Key Points You Should Say in Interview

* **Founded:** 1968
* **Headquarters:** Mumbai, India
* **Global Presence:** Operates in 50+ countries
* **CEO (recent):** K. Krithivasan

---

### What TCS Does

TCS provides a wide range of services:

* Software development
* IT consulting
* Cloud computing
* Artificial Intelligence & Data Analytics
* Cybersecurity
* Business process outsourcing

It works with major global clients in industries like banking, healthcare, retail, and telecom.

---

### Why TCS is Famous

* One of the **largest IT companies in India**
* Known for **job stability and work culture**
* Strong focus on **learning & training (TCS iON, internal programs)**
* Major recruiter of freshers (TCS Ninja, Digital roles)
* Trusted brand under Tata Group
