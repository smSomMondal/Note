This is a very common **HR interview question**, and they don’t expect an exact prediction—they want to see your **goals, ambition, and commitment**.

Here’s a strong, professional answer you can use (especially for IT / TCS / tech roles):

---

### ✅ Sample Answer (Best for Freshers / IT Students)

> “In the next five years, I see myself growing into a skilled and reliable professional in this organization. I want to build strong technical expertise, especially in areas like software development and problem-solving.
>
> I also aim to take on more responsibilities, contribute to important projects, and gradually move into a position where I can guide or mentor others.
>
> Most importantly, I want to continuously learn and stay updated with new technologies so I can add value to the company.”

---

### 💡 Why this answer works:

* Shows **long-term commitment** (important for companies like TCS)
* Highlights **learning mindset**
* Mentions **growth + contribution**
* Avoids unrealistic answers (like “I want to be CEO” 😄)

---

### 🔥 Short Version (if interviewer wants quick answer)

> “In five years, I see myself as a technically strong professional, contributing to key projects and growing into a responsible role while continuously learning new technologies.”

---

### ⚠️ Avoid these mistakes:

* ❌ “I don’t know”
* ❌ “I want to switch companies”
* ❌ “I want a high salary only”
* ❌ Unrealistic goals (CEO, manager immediately)
